Welcome to **xBNC**.

We build tools that keep IRC simple, secure and always within reach. Our main project is **[xBNC](https://github.com/x-bnc/xbnc)**, an IRC bouncer written in Rust that stays connected to your networks while you are away and gives you everything you missed on any of your devices.

### What we care about

- **Open source.** Everything we publish is free to use, study and improve, under the Apache License 2.0.
- **Security first.** Careful design, written threat models and responsible disclosure of vulnerabilities.
- **Made for people.** Clear interfaces, documentation in English and Spanish, and guides for those who are new to IRC or to running a server.
- **Respect for IRC.** We work with the networks and clients people already use, from the classic ones to the most modern IRCv3 features.

### Get involved

Try our projects, report what you find in their issues and help us translate them into your language. Every report and every idea makes them better.
